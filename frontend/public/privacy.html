Education Beyond Borders

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Education Beyond Borders ("EBB", "we") is a STEM Muslims project that records and translates free educational videos. This policy explains what personal information the EBB platform at ebb.stemmuslims.com collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. The platform is used by EBB volunteers (admins, teachers and translators), and accounts are created by invitation only.

Information we collect

  • Account details that an EBB admin enters for you: your name, email address, phone number, your roles (teacher and/or translator), the subjects you teach and the languages you translate.
  • Sign-in information. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, basic profile information and your Google account ID, which we use only to confirm who you are. If you sign in with a password, we store only a secure (bcrypt) hash of it, never the password itself.
  • Profile picture, if you choose to upload one.
  • Content you upload: lesson videos and caption (subtitle) files.
  • Activity records: which tasks you claim, release or complete, and when.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the platform: to let you sign in, match you to recording and translation tasks, show who is working on what, and publish the finished educational videos. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers listed below.

Where your information is stored

We use the following service providers to host the platform. Each processes data on our behalf:

  • a managed PostgreSQL database provider (account details and activity records);
  • Koyeb (our backend server) and Netlify (this website);
  • Amazon Web Services S3 (uploaded videos, caption files and profile pictures);
  • Google / YouTube (sign-in with Google, and publishing lesson videos).

YouTube

EBB uses YouTube API Services. Lesson videos uploaded to the platform are published to EBB's own YouTube channel, usually as unlisted videos. We never access or post to your personal YouTube account. When a video is taken down on the platform, it is made private on YouTube. YouTube's handling of these videos is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google user data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can remove EBB's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. After you sign in, your browser's local storage keeps a sign-in token so you stay logged in; signing out removes it. The site loads its font from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it

We keep your account details for as long as you volunteer with EBB, and delete them on request. Published lesson videos stay in the EBB library unless they are taken down.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email us at stemmus@ic.ac.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
